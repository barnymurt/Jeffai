# ETSY SHOP SETUP CHECKLIST
*Complete configuration guide - 30 minutes total*

## STEP 1: Create Your Etsy Account (5 minutes)
1. Go to etsy.com/sell
2. Sign up with email (use professional email address)
3. Verify email address
4. Choose "I'm selling handmade/vintage/craft supplies" → **Select: "Craft supplies & tools"**

---

## STEP 2: Shop Basics (10 minutes)

### Shop Name Ideas (Choose One):
- **Recommended:** "PlannerProCo" 
- **Alternatives:** "OrganizedLifeCo" / "PrintableProPlanners" / "PlannerStudioDesigns"

**Check Availability:** Shop name must be unique on Etsy

### Shop Location:
- Select your actual country/state for tax purposes
- This affects payment processing and legal requirements

### Shop Language & Currency:
- Language: English
- Currency: USD (or your local currency)

---

## STEP 3: Shop Branding & Appearance (10 minutes)

### Shop Banner (1200 x 300 pixels):
**Quick Creation in Canva:**
1. Search "Etsy Banner" template
2. Text: "[Your Shop Name] | Digital Planners & Printable Templates"
3. Subtitle: "Instant Downloads • Professional Designs • Lifetime Access"
4. Color scheme: Match your product colors (suggest: navy blue + white + gold accent)
5. Download as PNG

### Shop Icon/Logo (400 x 400 pixels):
- Simple text logo with shop name
- Same colors as banner
- Clean, readable font

### Shop Announcement (160 characters):
```
🎯 Professional printable planners & templates for organized living! Instant downloads, lifetime access. New designs added weekly!
```

## STEP 5: Payment & Billing Setup

### Payment Methods:
- **Enable:** PayPal, Etsy Payments (credit cards)
- **Processing Fee:** ~3-5% (built into Etsy)
- **Payout Schedule:** Choose weekly or monthly

### Tax Information:
- **US Sellers:** Provide SSN or EIN for tax reporting
- **International:** Follow your country's requirements
- **Sales Tax:** Etsy handles this automatically in most jurisdictions

---

## STEP 6: Shipping Profiles (For Digital Downloads)

### Create "Digital Download" Profile:
1. Go to Shop Manager → Settings → Shipping Settings
2. Create new shipping profile: "Digital Downloads"
3. **Processing Time:** Ready to ship in 1 business day
4. **Shipping Cost:** FREE (since it's digital)
5. **Delivery Method:** Electronic delivery
6. Apply this profile to all your listings
