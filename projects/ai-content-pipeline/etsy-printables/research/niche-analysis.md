## 💰 Pricing Strategy Analysis

### **Etsy Fee Structure (2025/2026):**
- **Listing Fee:** $0.20 per item
- **Transaction Fee:** 6.5% of total sale
- **Payment Processing:** 3% + $0.25 per transaction
- **Total Fees:** ~10-12% of sale price

### **Optimal Pricing Sweet Spots:**
- **Single Items:** $3.99-9.99 (after fees: $3.20-8.40 profit)
- **Small Bundles:** $9.99-19.99 (after fees: $8.40-17.20 profit)
- **Large Bundles:** $19.99-49.99 (after fees: $17.20-43.20 profit)

**Key Insight:** Avoid $1-2 pricing - fees eat profits. $4+ minimum recommended.

3. **Cross-Niche Bundles** - Combining categories (planner + wall art)
