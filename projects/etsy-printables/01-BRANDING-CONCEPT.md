- Small business owners
- Parents managing family finances
- Anyone wanting better financial organization

**Value Proposition:**
- Instant download convenience
- Professional, clean designs
- Practical, tested templates
- Affordable compared to hiring planners/coaches

## BRAND PERSONALITY
- **Professional** but approachable
- **Clean** but not sterile
- **Practical** over pretty
- **Trustworthy** and reliable

## VISUAL IDENTITY
**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep Blue (#1E40AF) - trust, professionalism
- Secondary: Teal (#0D9488) - growth, balance
- Accent: Warm Gray (#6B7280) - sophistication
- White (#FFFFFF) for clean backgrounds

**Typography:**
- Headers: Modern sans-serif (clean, readable)
- Body: Clean sans-serif for maximum readability
- Accent: Light script for personality touches

**Style Guidelines:**
- Minimalist layouts with plenty of white space
- Professional color blocking
- Clean icons and dividers
- Consistent margins and spacing
