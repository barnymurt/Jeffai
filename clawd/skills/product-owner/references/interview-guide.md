# Story-Based Interview Guide

## Core Principle

Ask for specific past experiences, never hypotheticals.

**Why:** People are bad at predicting their own behavior. Past behavior is the best predictor of future behavior.

## The Story-Based Technique

### Opening Questions

Start broad, then narrow to specifics:

- "Tell me about the last time you [relevant activity]"
- "Walk me through a recent time when you needed to [task]"
- "Can you describe a specific situation where [problem] happened?"

### Follow-Up Questions

Dig deeper into the story:

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| "What happened before that?" | Understand context and triggers |
| "What were you trying to accomplish?" | Uncover underlying goals |
| "What made that difficult?" | Identify pain points |
| "How did you solve that?" | Learn current workarounds |
| "What would have made it easier?" | Surface opportunity space |
| "What happened next?" | Understand consequences |
| "How often does this happen?" | Gauge frequency/severity |

### Probing Deeper

When you hear something interesting:

- "Tell me more about that"
- "What do you mean by [term they used]?"
- "You mentioned X — can you give me a specific example?"
- "How did that make you feel?"
- "What did you do about it?"

## What NOT to Ask

### Avoid Hypotheticals
- ❌ "Would you use a feature that does X?"
- ❌ "How much would you pay for Y?"
- ❌ "Do you think you would like Z?"

### Avoid Leading Questions
- ❌ "Don't you think it would be better if...?"
- ❌ "Wouldn't you agree that...?"
- ❌ "Most people say X — what do you think?"

### Avoid Future Predictions
- ❌ "Will you use this next week?"
- ❌ "How often do you think you'd use it?"

## Interview Structure

### 1. Warm-up (2-3 min)
- Thank them for their time
- Explain purpose: "We're trying to understand how people [activity]"
- Set expectations: "There are no right or wrong answers"

### 2. Background (3-5 min)
- "Tell me a bit about yourself and your role"
- "How long have you been doing [activity]?"
- "What tools do you currently use for [task]?"

### 3. Story Collection (15-20 min)
- Ask for 2-3 specific stories
- Use follow-up questions to dig deep
- Take notes on exact words they use

### 4. Opportunity Exploration (5-10 min)
- "What's the hardest part about [activity]?"
- "If you had a magic wand, what would you change?"
- "What have you tried that didn't work?"

### 5. Wrap-up (2-3 min)
- "Is there anything else you think I should know?"
- "Can I follow up if I have more questions?"
- Thank them again

## Documenting Insights

After each interview, capture:

1. **Direct quotes** - Their exact words (use quotation marks)
2. **Opportunities** - Pain points and unmet needs
3. **Current behavior** - How they solve problems today
4. **Emotional moments** - Where they showed frustration/delight
5. **Surprises** - Anything unexpected

## Sample Interview Script

**Opening:**
"Thanks for taking the time to chat. I'm trying to understand how people manage their [task]. There are no right or wrong answers — I just want to learn from your experience."

**Story prompt:**
"Can you tell me about the last time you had to [specific activity]? Walk me through what happened."

**Follow-ups:**
- "What were you trying to accomplish?"
- "What tools or resources did you use?"
- "What was the hardest part?"
- "How did it turn out?"
- "How often do you run into this?"

**Closing:**
"This has been really helpful. Is there anything else about [topic] that you think I should know?"
