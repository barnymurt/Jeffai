# General Internal Communications

Use this format for status reports, leadership updates, project updates, incident reports, and other internal communications that don't fit the specific 3P/newsletter/FAQ formats.

## Format Structure

**Subject Line** - Clear, specific, action-oriented
**Opening** - Context and purpose of communication
**Main Content** - Key information organized logically  
**Action Items** - What needs to happen and by whom
**Next Steps** - Timeline and follow-up plan
**Contact Info** - Who to reach out to with questions

## Tone Guidelines
- Professional but approachable
- Get to the point quickly
- Use clear headers and bullet points
- Anticipate reader questions
- Include specific dates and action items
- End with clear next steps
