# Company Newsletter

Use this format for company-wide communications and newsletters.

## Format Structure

**Opening** - Brief welcome/intro
**Company News** - Major announcements, wins, milestones  
**Team Highlights** - Recognition, new hires, achievements
**Product/Project Updates** - Key developments and progress
**Culture & Community** - Events, initiatives, fun content
**Looking Ahead** - What's coming up, key dates

## Tone Guidelines
- Warm and inclusive
- Celebrate achievements and people
- Balance professional with personable
- Use engaging language that builds community
- Include visuals/emojis where appropriate
