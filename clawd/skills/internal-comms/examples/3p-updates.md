# 3P Updates (Progress, Plans, Problems)

Use this format for team status updates that follow the Progress/Plans/Problems structure.

## Format Structure

**Progress** 📈
- What did we accomplish this week/period?
- Key wins and milestones hit
- Metrics and measurable outcomes

**Plans** 🎯  
- What are we focusing on next week/period?
- Key priorities and objectives
- Timeline and deliverables

**Problems** ⚠️
- What blockers or challenges are we facing?
- Risks that need attention
- Areas where we need help or support

## Tone Guidelines
- Concise and factual
- Highlight wins but be honest about challenges
- Action-oriented language
- Use bullet points for readability

## Template

Subject: [Team/Project] - Week of [Date] 3P Update

**Progress** 📈
- [Key accomplishment 1]
- [Key accomplishment 2]  
- [Metrics/outcomes]

**Plans** 🎯
- [Priority 1 for next period]
- [Priority 2 for next period]
- [Timeline/deliverables]

**Problems** ⚠️
- [Blocker/challenge 1]
- [Support needed]
- [Risk to highlight]

---
Next update: [Date]
