## 💰 Current Market Pricing (2025/2026 Data)

### Traditional Content Marketing Agencies

**Blog Posts:**
- US: $100-500 per 1,000-word post
- UK: £75-400 per 1,000-word post
- Factors: Length, expertise, SEO optimization, research depth

**Social Media Content:**
- US: $50-150 per post
- UK: £40-120 per post  
- Bundle packages: 10 posts/month at fixed pricing

**Video Content:**
- US: $1,000+ per video (basic)
- UK: £800+ per video
- High-end productions: $3,000-7,000+ per video

**Infographics:**
- US: $300-1,000
- UK: £250-800

**E-books/Whitepapers:**
- US: $1,500+ 
- UK: £1,200+

**Podcast Production:**
- US: $500-3,000 per episode
- UK: £400-2,500 per episode

### AI Content Tools (Individual/Self-Service)

**Software-as-a-Service Tools:**
- **Jasper AI:** $49-249/month (Creator to Advanced plans)
- **Copy.ai:** Free tier + paid plans starting ~$49/month
- **Writesonic:** $20-499/month (Basic to Advanced)

**Key Limitations:** These are DIY tools requiring user expertise, limited customization, no strategy/implementation service

   - Limited text + image + video + automation integration
   - No comprehensive content ecosystem approach
