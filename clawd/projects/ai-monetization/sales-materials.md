## 📞 Sales Call Framework

### **Discovery Questions (First 10 Minutes)**

**Current State Assessment:**
1. "What's your current content marketing setup?"
2. "How much are you spending monthly on content creation?"
3. "What's your biggest content challenge right now?"
4. "How long does it typically take to get content published?"
5. "What content types drive the best results for you?"

**Pain Point Identification:**
1. "What happens when your content team gets overwhelmed?"
2. "How do you ensure brand consistency across all content?"
3. "What's preventing you from creating more content?"
4. "How do you measure content ROI currently?"
5. "What would doubling your content output mean for your business?"

### **Solution Presentation (Next 15 Minutes)**

**Problem-Solution Match:**
- Mirror their specific challenges back to them
- Show exactly how our service addresses each pain point
- Use relevant portfolio examples for their industry
- Demonstrate ROI with specific numbers

**Proof Points:**
- Share case study metrics from similar companies
- Explain our quality control process
- Walk through typical deliverables for their tier
- Show timeline comparison (us vs. alternatives)

### **Objection Handling Scripts**

**"We're happy with our current agency"**
*"That's great to hear! What if you could get the same quality at 60% less cost and 10x faster? Would that be worth a conversation about freeing up budget for other marketing initiatives?"*

**"AI content isn't high quality"**
*"I understand that concern - most AI tools produce generic content. That's why we combine AI efficiency with human strategy and oversight. Let me show you a sample of content we've created that most people assume was written entirely by humans."*

**"We don't have budget for more content"**
*"Actually, if you're spending more than $500/month on any content creation, we can likely save you money while increasing your output. Can I show you a quick cost comparison?"*

**"We need to think about it"**
*"Absolutely, this is an important decision. What specific information would help you move forward? I can prepare a custom analysis of your current content costs vs. our service."*

### **Closing Techniques**

**Trial Close:**
*"Based on what you've shared, it sounds like the Growth package would be perfect for your needs. Would you like to start with a 30-day pilot to see the results?"*

**Urgency Close:**
*"I can offer you our beta pricing for the first 90 days - that's 20% off the regular rate. This offer is only available for the next 10 clients as we build out our case studies."*

**Risk Reversal:**
*"Let's do this - if after 30 days you're not seeing at least a 3x improvement in your content output and quality, I'll personally refund your entire first month. Fair enough?"*
