# DESIGN BRIEFS - Quick Creation Guide
*Simple, professional designs that sell*

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES FOR ALL PRODUCTS

### Style Guidelines:
- **Clean & Minimal:** White backgrounds, plenty of white space
- **Professional Typography:** Sans-serif fonts (Montserrat, Open Sans, Lato)
- **Consistent Color Palette:** 2-3 colors maximum per design
- **Easy to Print:** Black text on white, avoid heavy ink usage
- **Mobile-Friendly Preview:** Designs must look good in small Etsy thumbnails

### Color Schemes (Create 3 versions of each):
1. **Classic:** Black text, white background, one accent color
2. **Soft:** Pastels (blush pink, sage green, soft blue)  
3. **Bold:** Vibrant but professional (teal, coral, golden yellow)

## PRODUCT 1: Monthly Budget Planner
**Time Limit: 2 hours**

### Page Layout (6 pages total):

#### Page 1: Cover Page
- **Title:** "Monthly Budget Planner" (large, centered)
- **Subtitle:** "Month: _______ Year: _______"
- **Design Elements:** Simple border, small decorative element (geometric shape)
- **Footer:** Small motivational quote about financial goals

#### Page 2: Monthly Overview
**Layout:** Two-column format
- **Left Column:**
  - "Monthly Income" section with 5 line items
  - Total Income box
- **Right Column:**  
  - "Fixed Expenses" (rent, utilities, loans) - 8 line items
  - "Variable Expenses" (groceries, entertainment, etc.) - 8 line items
  - Total Expenses box
- **Bottom:** Net Income calculation (Income - Expenses)
- **Design:** Simple boxes with lines for writing, clear headers

#### Page 3: Monthly Goals & Priorities
- **Top Section:** "This Month's Financial Goals" (3 goal boxes)
- **Middle:** "Savings Targets" (emergency fund, vacation, etc.)
- **Bottom:** "Focus Areas" checkboxes (debt payoff, increase income, reduce spending)

#### Pages 4-7: Weekly Breakdown (4 identical pages)
- **Header:** "Week [1/2/3/4] - Dates: _____ to _____"
- **Daily sections:** 7 boxes for each day of the week
- **Each day box includes:**
  - Date line
  - "Spent: $____"
  - 3-4 lines for expense details
- **Weekly Total:** Bottom calculation box
- **Notes section:** Right side for weekly observations

#### Page 8: Monthly Reflection
- **Top:** "Monthly Review - How Did I Do?"
- **Sections:**
  - "Goals Achieved" (checkboxes)
  - "Biggest Wins"
  - "Areas to Improve"
  - "Next Month's Priority"
  - "Lessons Learned"

### Design Specifications:
- **Size:** 8.5" x 11" (US Letter)
- **Margins:** 0.75" on all sides
- **Font Sizes:** Headers 16-18pt, subheaders 12-14pt, body text 10-11pt
- **Grid System:** Use consistent spacing throughout

## PRODUCT 5: Daily Schedule Template
**Time Limit: 35 minutes**

### Single Page Layout:
- **Header:** "Daily Planner - Date: _______ Day: _______"
- **Left Side (60% of page):** Hourly schedule
  - Time blocks from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM
  - 30-minute increments shown
  - Clean lines for writing appointments/tasks
- **Right Side (40% of page):**
  - "Today's Priorities" (top 3 tasks)
  - "Important Calls/Meetings"
  - "Notes & Ideas" section
  - "Tomorrow's Prep" section at bottom

### Time Block Design:
- **Hour markers:** Bold (8:00 AM)
- **Half-hour markers:** Light line (8:30 AM)
- **Writing space:** Adequate room for 2-3 lines per hour

## QUICK DESIGN TOOLS & TIPS

### Recommended Software:
1. **Canva Pro** (easiest, templates available)
2. **Adobe Illustrator** (professional, if you have it)
3. **Google Docs/Slides** (free option with tables)
4. **Figma** (free, web-based)

### Canva Shortcuts:
- Search "planner templates" for starting points
- Use "Brand Kit" to keep colors consistent
- "Magic Resize" to create multiple versions quickly
- Download as high-quality PDF (300 DPI)

### Font Recommendations (All Free):
- **Headers:** Montserrat Bold, Open Sans Bold
- **Body Text:** Open Sans Regular, Lato Regular  
- **Accent Text:** Raleway Light, Source Sans Pro

### Color Palette Generators:
- Coolors.co for instant palettes
- Adobe Color for professional schemes
- Keep hex codes noted for consistency

### Design Quality Checklist:
- ✅ High contrast (dark text on light background)
- ✅ Readable font sizes (minimum 10pt)
- ✅ Consistent spacing and alignment
- ✅ Print-friendly (not too much ink usage)
- ✅ Professional appearance (no Comic Sans!)
- ✅ Clear hierarchy (headers bigger than body text)

### File Export Settings:
- **Format:** PDF (for Etsy)
- **Quality:** High/300 DPI minimum
- **Size:** 8.5" x 11" (US Letter standard)
- **File Size:** Under 40MB (Etsy limit)

*Remember: Simple and functional beats overly decorative. Your customers want tools that work, not art pieces.*
